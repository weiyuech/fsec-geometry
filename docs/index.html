How to read the measurement

The push. French comes from matched English and French prompts. Tense comes from matched past and future prompts. We combine their directions and rotate the activation at the last token of a separate five-token text.

The boundary. We measure the Euclidean distance between the original and perturbed layer-40 activations. The solid line marks a distance of on the median response grid for 30 texts. The same threshold applies in every quadrant.

The units. Each half-axis is scaled so its measured intercept is one. A 45° line mixes equal fractions of those intercepts. In these coordinates, the dashed circle reaches one unit in every direction.

The signs. The original two-direction sweep sampled the shaded upper-right quadrant. The other three quarters come from reversing one or both directions in this run.
